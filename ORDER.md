## Buyer Inputs Needed

- Completed `buyer-brief-template.md`.
- Business name and service area.
- Services to include.
- Approved contact or booking link.
- Approved proof, testimonials, photos, or claim limits.
- Publishing or handoff preference.

## Acceptance Checklist

- Buyer confirms all business facts and assets are authorized.
- Buyer accepts the fixed $200 scope.
- Buyer uses an external payment route controlled by the seller.
- Work starts only after payment or funded milestone is authorized.
- Final delivery includes customized HTML and handoff notes.

Do not post private customer data, payment screenshots, credentials, or sensitive business files in GitHub issues.
